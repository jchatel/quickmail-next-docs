# Renewing Google Domains
When purchasing Google email accounts through QuickMail, you’ll need to purchase a new domain for those accounts. Domains are registered for one year and will expire unless renewed. If you’d like to keep using the domain and its associated email accounts, be sure to renew it before the expiration date.

## How to Renew Google Domains?

You’ll receive email notifications 60, 30, 7, and 1 day before your domain expires.

A banner prompting you to renew the domain will also appear in your QuickMail account a few days after you receive the first 60-day email notification.

## What happens if I'm unable to renew the domains on time?

Unfortunately, once the domains expire, they can no longer be reactivated. This cannot be bypassed by our support team.

**Note:** Expired domains are not immediately available for purchase again. Depending on the domain extension and registrar, they may become available after approximately 30–80 days, but availability is not guaranteed.
