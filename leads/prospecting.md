# QuickMail's Prospecting 🔍

QuickMail Prospecting gives you free access to our lead database, allowing you to search and filter prospects directly within QuickMail to quickly find new leads for your outreach campaigns.

To use Prospecting, you'll need to install the **QuickMail LinkedIn Browser Extension**, which is required to retrieve lead information from LinkedIn.

## How to Use QuickMail Prospecting? 

**Step 1:** Install the QuickMail LinkedIn Browser Extension. Here's a fuide [Linked Browser Extension](https://help.quickmail.com/linkedin/quickmails-linkedin-browser-extension/)

**Step 2:** Go to the **Prospecting** page in QuickMail.

**Step 3:** Use the available filters to find leads that match your desired criteria.

**Step 4:** Select the leads you'd like to import. 

**Step 5:** Choose the campaign where you'd like to add the selected leads, then import them.
