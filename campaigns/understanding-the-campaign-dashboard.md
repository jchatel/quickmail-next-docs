# Understanding the Campaign Dashboard and Status

QuickMail's campaign dashboard gives you a clear overview of your campaign's performance and current status. Use it to review key stats, track progress, and spot areas that may need attention.

## Analyzing Your Campaign Dashboard
Here's what you can see in each part of the dashboard and how to use it.

### Campaign Stats

The campaign stats give you the overall campaign results at a glance, including the campaign's open rate, click rate, accepted connection request rate, unsubscribe rate, and bounce rate.

The detailed campaign stats give you a view of your daily performance, including stats for each campaign step.

### Campaign Progress

The campaign progress bar gives you an overview of how many leads are still in progress, have not yet started, or have already completed the campaign.

You can also see how many leads are on a specific step by clicking the drop-down button.
