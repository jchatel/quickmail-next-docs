# Analyzing Campaign Dashboard and Status

This guide explains how to determine where your leads are in a campaign
and how many emails have been sent.

## Where are the leads?

Use the **Campaign Dashboard** to understand the current status of your
leads.

Typical lead statuses include:

-   **Not Started** -- The lead has been added but has not entered the
    campaign yet.
-   **In Progress** -- The lead is currently moving through the campaign
    steps.
-   **Waiting** -- The lead is waiting for the next scheduled step or
    delay period.
-   **Replied** -- The lead has replied and is no longer progressing
    through the campaign.
-   **Finished** -- The lead has completed all campaign steps.

Review the campaign metrics and lead list to identify where leads are
currently located in the sequence.

## How many emails have been sent?

The Campaign Dashboard displays email performance metrics, including:

-   Total emails sent
-   Emails delivered
-   Opens
-   Clicks (if tracking is enabled)
-   Replies
-   Bounces

These metrics provide an overview of campaign performance and the total
number of emails sent for the selected campaign.

## Tips

-   Verify that your campaign is active and has sending inboxes
    assigned.
-   Ensure leads have been added successfully.
-   Check campaign schedules if leads appear to be waiting.
-   Review campaign filters when analyzing lead progress.
