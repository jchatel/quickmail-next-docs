# How to enable or disable open and click notifications

Open and click notifications, including notification sounds, are enabled by default in QuickMail.

To change these settings:

1. Log in to your QuickMail account.
2. Click the **ellipsis (three dots)** in the upper-right corner of the screen.
3. Toggle the notification settings you want to change:
   - Turn them **off** to disable notifications or sounds.
   - Turn them **on** to enable notifications or sounds again.

You can adjust the open and click notification settings to suit your preferences.
